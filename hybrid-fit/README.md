# HybridFit

**Effortless Workout Planning for Hybrid Athletes**

HybridFit helps you train toward your hybrid goal, integrating sport, running, and strength training seamlessly. Whether you're a beginner starting your fitness journey or an advanced athlete optimizing your performance, HybridFit provides the tools, guidance, and tracking you need to achieve your goals.

---

## What is HybridFit?

HybridFit is a comprehensive fitness platform designed for hybrid athletes who want to combine multiple training disciplines. Our platform offers structured training plans, an extensive exercise library, detailed workout tracking, and intelligent progress monitoring to help you become a well-rounded athlete.

The platform is perfect for anyone who wants to:
- Train for a specific sport while maintaining strength
- Combine running with strength training
- Follow structured programs that adapt to their schedule
- Track detailed workout metrics and see progress over time
- Access a vast library of exercises and drills

### 📊 Workout Tracking & Logging

Log every workout with detailed metrics to build a comprehensive record of your training journey.

**Workout Status Options:**
- **Completed**: Mark workouts you've finished
- **Skipped**: Intentionally skipped workouts (with optional notes explaining why)
- **Missed**: Workouts that were planned but not completed

**For Distance Workouts (Running, Cycling, etc.):**
- **Distance**: Log miles or kilometers covered
- **Duration**: Record total workout time in minutes
- **Pace**: Track average pace per mile or kilometer
- **Heart Rate**: Optional average heart rate monitoring
- **Perceived Effort**: Rate your effort level from 1-10
- **Notes**: Add observations, how you felt, or any modifications

**For Strength Training Workouts:**
- **Exercise Tracking**: Log each exercise with sets, reps, and weight
- **Volume Calculation**: Automatic total volume calculation (weight × reps × sets)
- **Set Management**: Add or remove sets as needed
- **Completion Status**: Mark individual sets as completed
- **Exercise Customization**: Modify exercise names or add custom exercises
- **Duration & Effort**: Track workout duration and perceived effort

**For Drill-Based Workouts (Soccer, Sport-Specific):**
- **Drill Tracking**: Log each drill with duration, repetitions, and sets
- **Quality Rating**: Rate drill execution quality from 1-5
- **Completion Status**: Mark drills as completed
- **Custom Metrics**: Add notes about player count, modifications, or observations
- **Duration Tracking**: Record time spent on each drill

**For Mixed Workouts:**
- **Combined Tracking**: Log both strength exercises and drills in the same session
- **Tabbed Interface**: Easy switching between exercise types
- **Comprehensive Metrics**: All metrics available for mixed training sessions

**Additional Tracking Features:**
- **Edit Previous Logs**: Update any past workout log with new information
- **Notes Field**: Add detailed notes about how the workout felt, what went well, or areas for improvement
- **Perceived Effort Scale**: Standardized 1-10 effort rating for consistent tracking across all workout types
